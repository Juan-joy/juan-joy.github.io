Morning breath starts while you sleep.

Overnight, your mouth gets drier and the bacteria on your tongue and gums keep working. They release sulfur compounds, the cause of that familiar smell, and by morning it has had hours to build.

Brushing before bed helps, but you can't brush while you sleep. Light Patches is built to work the night shift.

One patch, one night.

The idea is simple: put it on at bedtime, then get on with sleeping.

  1. Before bed

    After you brush, place one patch inside your mouth. It's bio-adhesive, so it's designed to stay put.

  2. While you sleep

    The patch releases slowly, so it keeps working for hours after you've fallen asleep.

  3. In the morning

    Wake up and get on with your day, with nothing extra to remember.

Why a patch?

Hands-free

Once it's on, you're done. No alarm, no routine to keep up at 2 am.

Slow release

Sprays and mints freshen for a moment. A patch is designed to work steadily over hours.

Small and discreet

Slim enough to forget about, and made for the beauty shelf rather than the medicine cabinet.
